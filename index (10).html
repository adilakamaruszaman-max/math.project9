                    Case Study
                    Elaeis guineensis (Tenera variety)
                

                
The Bioscience Problem

                

                    In Malaysia, Elaeis guineensis is the powerhouse of the agricultural sector. For an Agro Product Technologist, determining the exact harvest window is critical. Harvesting too early results in low oil yield, while harvesting too late leads to fruit drop and rapid buildup of undesirable Free Fatty Acids (FFA).
                

                

                    Lipid accumulation inside the fruit's fleshy outer layer (the mesocarp) follows a sigmoidal (S-shaped) logistic curve. By using the derivative \( f'(x) \) of this curve, we can mathematically calculate the precise rate of oil accumulation (synthesis velocity) week-by-week to identify optimal estate harvesting cycles.
                

                

                    
                    

                        Malaysian Benchmark: Industry standards based on 
                        Malaysian Palm Oil Board (MPOB) 
                        state rapid lipid deposition initiates around 14–15 WAA (Weeks After Anthesis) and plateaus by 20–22 WAA.
                    

                

            


            
            

                

                     Mathematical Models
                

                
                
                

                    

                        Oil Content Function
                        f(x)
                    

                    
Calculates accumulated oil content as % of dry mesocarp weight at week \( x \):

                    

                        \[ f(x) = \frac{L}{1 + e^{-k(x - x_0)}} \]
                    

                


                
                

                    

                        Oil Accumulation Rate
                        f'(x)
                    

                    
Calculates rate of oil synthesis in percentage points per week:

                    

                        \[ f'(x) = \frac{L \cdot k \cdot e^{-k(x - x_0)}}{(1 + e^{-k(x - x_0)})^2} \]
                    

                


                
                

                    
L: Max potential oil (~80% dry wt)

                    
k: Growth rate scaling factor

                    
x₀: Synthesis inflection point (mid-ripening)

                    
x: Weeks After Anthesis (WAA)
